# Dashboard

The Dashboard is the main hub for Free Flight Mode.

Use it to see what to fly next, check your free-tier airline access, review your pilot stats, and jump to the main Free Flight pages.

Career Mode has its own Career Dashboard and progression pages.

## What the Dashboard shows

The Dashboard changes based on your current status.

It can show:

- Your next booked flight.
- Your active flight.
- A recovery option after a crash or outage.
- Your pending roster.
- Free-tier airline rotation.
- Monthly airline choice.
- Pilot rank and XP progress.
- Operations stats.
- Global ranking preview.
- Quick links to important pages.

## Next Mission

The Next Mission strip shows the most important action right now.

| State | Button | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No booked flight | Find a Flight | Opens Schedules so you can book a route. |
| Upcoming flight | Initiate Boarding | Opens My Flights so you can start the next booking. |
| Active flight | Resume Mission | Opens Active Flight so you can continue tracking or finish. |
| Recoverable flight | Recover Flight | Opens the Recovery Wizard for an interrupted online flight. |

FlyHub prioritizes active flights first. If you have an active flight and several upcoming flights, the active flight is shown as the next mission.

## Boarding Pass

The Boarding Pass card shows the next active or upcoming flight.

It can include:

- Airline logo and name.
- Flight number.
- Active or scheduled status.
- Historical badge when the booking is historical.
- Aircraft type.
- Origin and destination ICAO.
- Airport names.
- Route distance.
- Departure and arrival METARs when available.

If there is no booking, the Dashboard shows an empty boarding pass state and sends you to Schedules.

## Pending Roster

The Pending Roster shows up to five upcoming or active Free Flight bookings.

Each row shows:

- Queue position.
- Route.
- Flight number.
- Aircraft.
- Status.

Click a roster entry to open My Flights.

## Free-tier airline access

Free users see an Operational Access section.

This section shows:

- Five weekly rotating airlines.
- Countdown to the next weekly rotation.
- One monthly personal airline choice.
- Locked state for airlines outside your free access.

The weekly rotation refreshes every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

Each weekly airline card can take you to Schedules filtered for that airline.

## Monthly airline choice

Cadet users can choose one extra airline for the current UTC month.

If you have not chosen one yet:

1. Open Dashboard.
2. Find the Monthly Personal Airline section.
3. Select the airline you want.
4. Click Lock Airline.

After you lock the monthly airline, it stays available until the next UTC month.

You cannot keep changing the monthly airline during the same UTC month.

## Premium prompt

Free users may see a premium card for Captain features.

Captain unlocks:

- All active airlines.
- Historical Routes.
- Manual Flights.
- VIP Ops.
- Career Mode.
- Unrestricted scheduler access.
- Priority support.
- Captain role and profile badge.

## Operations Overview

The Dashboard includes operations stats such as:

- Flight hours.
- Distance this month.
- Active bids.
- Total XP.
- Recent live activity.

Use this section as a quick health check for your Free Flight activity.

## Pilot License

The right-side pilot card shows:

- Avatar.
- Display name.
- Rank.
- Total flights.
- Average score.
- XP progress toward the next rank.

## Global Rankings

The Dashboard can show a preview of top pilots.

Use it to compare Free Flight performance and jump to the full [Leaderboard](/leaderboard).

## Quick Links

Quick Links help you jump directly to common Free Flight pages:

- Schedules.
- My Flights.
- Live Map.
- Leaderboard or rankings.

## Leaving while a flight is active

If you try to leave Free Flight while an active flight exists, FlyHub may ask you what to do.

You can:

- Keep tracking and stay in the current flow.
- Reset and leave, which moves active Free Flight bookings back to My Flights when possible.

Do not reset unless you understand that the current active state may be cleared.

## Common mistakes

- Looking for Career assignments on the Free Flight Dashboard.
- Ignoring the weekly airline rotation as a free user.
- Forgetting that the monthly airline is locked for the UTC month.
- Expecting Recover Flight when no recent online recovery snapshot exists.
- Leaving Free Flight while a flight is active without reading the confirmation.
