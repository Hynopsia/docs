# Career Saves

Career Mode supports up to three separate saves on one FlyHub account. Each save has its own pilot, career progress, and in-game time.

## Open the career chooser

FlyHub keeps Free Flight Mode and Career Mode as two separate modes.

1. Open **Home**.
2. Choose **Enter Career Mode**.
3. Use the **Choose your career** screen to continue a save, select another save, or create a new one.

Opening Career Mode does not change your Free Flight profile or flights.

## Your three slots

You can use up to three Career slots. If you already had a Career before multiple saves were available, that Career is preserved in **Slot 1**.

Selecting **Create career** in an empty slot starts a new pilot from the beginning. You choose the pilot name, nationality, identity, difficulty, starter airline, training path, interview answers, base, and first contract again.

The following records belong to each save separately:

- Pilot name, nationality, identity, difficulty, and setup choices.
- Employer, rank, seniority, contracts, jobs, and assignments.
- Career calendar and scheduled flights.
- Logbook, flight statistics, visited airports, and visited countries in Passport.
- Achievements, type ratings, and pinned badges or ratings.
- Money, loans, properties, payroll, and other financial records.
- Reputation, training, fatigue, compliance, leave, notifications, and progression.

Your login, subscription, and account avatar are shared across all three saves.

## What the chooser shows

The **Choose your career** screen shows each slot and the actions available for it.

| Label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Active** | The save currently selected for Career Mode. Only one save can be active at a time. |
| **Last played** | The most recent date that save was opened. |
| **Continue** | Opens the active save and continues its current Career. |
| **Select** | Switches to another save. |
| **Create career** | Starts a new Career in an empty slot. |
| Trash button, then **Delete Career save** | Permanently removes that save after you review the warning. |

Other saves are paused. Look for **Active** to see which save is running.

## Active and paused saves

The selected save is **Active**. Its Career continues while FlyHub is closed, so offers, assignment deadlines, leave duration, inactivity checks, compliance timing, payroll timing, and similar Career timers can continue to move.

When you select another save, the save you leave becomes paused. Its Career time and timers stop while it is paused, including offer and assignment deadlines, vacation duration, inactivity and compliance timing, and payroll timing. A paused save does not build absence penalties simply because you are playing another save.

For example, if an offer in Save A has two days remaining and you select Save B, Save A keeps those two days while it is paused. Closing FlyHub with Save A active does not pause Save A.

Changes to available airlines and aircraft may still affect a save when you return.

Looking at another pilot's public profile or opening a public flight link does not switch your active Career save.

## Switch saves

Before switching, finish, cancel, or resolve any active Career flight or recovery item in the save you are leaving. FlyHub blocks a switch while that save has an active flight or unresolved recovery.

When the save is ready:

1. Return to **Home** and choose **Enter Career Mode**.
2. Select **Select** on the save you want to play.
3. Continue from the Career page that opens. A save with completed setup opens its Career Dashboard; a save still in setup opens onboarding.

The newly selected save becomes **Active** and the save you left becomes paused.

## Reset a save from Settings

Reset and delete are different actions.

**Reset Career Progress** works on the currently selected save. It clears that save's Career progress and sends the same slot back through full onboarding. The slot remains occupied, and your other saves are untouched.

To reset the selected save:

1. Open **Settings** and select **Career Mode**.
2. Under **Reset Career Progress**, choose **Reset Progress**.
3. Read the warning and type `reset my career` exactly as shown.
4. Choose **Reset selected Career save**.

Reset is permanent and has no undo. If the save has an active flight or unresolved recovery, finish, cancel, or resolve it before resetting.

## Delete a save from the chooser

Use the trash button on a save in **Choose your career**, review the warning, and choose **Delete Career save**. Deleting empties only that slot. It permanently removes that save's pilot, progress, records, and Career history, with no undo.

An active flight or unresolved recovery blocks deletion of that save. You can delete paused Save A while Save B is active. If you delete the active save, any remaining saves stay paused until you select one.

## Public profiles, leaderboards, and the map

Every save that has completed setup can appear independently in Career Leaderboards, including a paused save. If one account has three pilots with completed setup, each pilot can have its own leaderboard row.

Links from a Career leaderboard or public pilot page open the exact pilot and save represented by that link. Career Stats, Passport, achievements, type ratings, and pinned items show that save's records.

The public map remains public. A selected flight link opens the pilot who is flying it, so it does not replace the save you are currently playing.
