# Airline Profile: Pilots

The **Pilots** tab is the airline's active pilot roster. It shows who belongs to the airline or group and gives a compact view of each pilot's experience.

## What the roster shows

Each row can include:

- pilot name and avatar;
- current rank;
- date joined;
- completed flights with the airline;
- completed hours;
- average score out of 10 when available;
- date of the last completed flight.

**Active pilots** means pilots with an active membership. It does not necessarily mean they flew recently.

## Browse and contact pilots

The roster is paginated in groups of 30. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through it. Select a pilot name or **Profile** to open that pilot's public VA profile. Use **Message** to begin a conversation when messaging is available; the button is not shown for your own profile.

The roster currently has no search, sorting, or filter controls. It is a read-only directory; use [Inbox](./inbox) for communication and the management **People** area for administrative changes.

## Groups and visitors

Membership in the airline is not required to read its active roster. A visitor is shown as a visitor. An operating airline in a group may redirect to the flagship profile, where the roster is shared.

If there are no active pilots, the roster shows an empty state. If it cannot load, reload the profile or move to another tab and try again. A temporary roster error should not be read as a membership change.
