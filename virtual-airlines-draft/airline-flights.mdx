# Airline Profile: Flights

The **Flights** tab is a read-only board of flights associated with the airline. It is useful for seeing the airline's activity and opening a pilot or logbook record; it does not reserve a flight.

## Flight information

Rows can include:

- flight number;
- pilot;
- origin and destination;
- departure time in UTC;
- duration;
- aircraft;
- passenger or cargo load;
- score;
- current result or status.

The list focuses on the current week and the previous seven weeks. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move through the available weeks. Up to 20 flights are shown per page.

## Open related information

Select a pilot to open the pilot's public VA profile. When a logbook link is available, use it to inspect the corresponding record. A profile visitor can read the board without being a member of the airline.

The tab does not provide controls to edit, cancel, settle, or book another pilot's operation. Use [Dispatch](./dispatch) for your own new flight.

## Statuses

The interface can describe results such as scheduled, en route, landed, closed, cancelled, rejected, abandoned, expired, or flagged. The activity feed shows the statuses relevant to the selected period, so not every label appears every time.

## Groups and limits

For a group profile, the visible activity can include flights from its operating airlines. The tab does not provide filters to split that activity by operating airline. The page is paginated, but it does not expose every possible historical record in one view.

If the board cannot load, reload the profile and try again. A missing page of results is not a reason to submit a duplicate booking.
