# Virtual Airlines

Virtual Airlines is FlyHub's structured airline mode. Join an airline, operate its aircraft, follow its network, build a reputation, and take part in shared flights, events, charters, and tours.

This is the complete end-user guide: it explains not only what each page does, but also how the market, ticket prices, airline money, reputation, flight validation, and progression fit together. You can use the page-specific guides as a reference, then return to the foundation pages whenever a quote, result, or management decision needs more context.

This guide covers the complete pilot experience, public airline profiles, and the management workspace. Virtual Airlines is separate from Free Flight Mode and Career Mode.

## Before you begin

Virtual Airlines is available to every FlyHub user. Public pages can be browsed immediately; joining an airline, booking its flights, and managing an airline use the membership or manager role that applies to each action.

All dates and operating-day checks use UTC. When a page shows a limitation or an unavailable action, follow the guidance on that page before trying again.
