# Dashboard

The Virtual Airlines Dashboard is your starting point for the mode. It brings together your next operation, airline assignments, pilot progress, and recent activity.

## What you can see

- **Next operation**: the route, aircraft, and current state of your next VA flight.
- **Route and weather**: the planned route and relevant airport or weather information when available.
- **Upcoming roster**: a short view of upcoming operations.
- **Airline assignments**: flights or opportunities associated with your active airline memberships.
- **Pilot identity**: your VA rank, completed flights, hours, and experience progress.
- **Recent form**: a recent view of your flight performance.
- **Action center**: links to work that needs your attention.
- **Experience ranking**: your position in the available pilot ranking.

The Dashboard is a summary. Use [My Flights](./my-flights), [Calendar](./calendar), or [Dispatch](./dispatch) when you need to change or operate a flight.

## Common actions

### Open or recover a flight

Select the next operation to continue to the flight workflow. If FlyHub has a recoverable interrupted operation, use the recovery action and then review it in My Flights before continuing.

### Find an airline

Use the airline link to open your memberships or go to [Find Airlines](./find-airlines) to search for a new airline.

### Reset and leave

If you need to leave the current operation without discarding its setup, use **Reset and leave**. The operation returns to a reserved state while its aircraft, flight plan, and demand information are kept for the next attempt.

## Limits and refresh

Dashboard is your starting point for the mode. Its lists are intentionally short: for example, the page shows only a small set of upcoming assignments and recent flights. Open the related page for the complete list.

## If something is missing

The Dashboard can be useful even when you have no active airline membership, but airline-specific assignments will be empty. If a section cannot load, try the related page or reload the Dashboard. A temporary loading problem should not be treated as proof that your membership or flight has disappeared.
