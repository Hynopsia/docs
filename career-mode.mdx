# Career Mode

Career Mode is FlyHub's structured pilot progression mode.

Use it when you want a more game-like experience with career progression, airline employment, dispatch, contracts, money, and long-term consequences.

## How Career Mode is different

Free Flight Mode lets you pick routes freely.

Career Mode gives you a pilot career to manage. Your flights are part of a Career save, and your choices affect your progression.

## Career Mode is for players who want

- A structured pilot path.
- Career assignments.
- Airline or employer progression.
- Contracts.
- Career dispatch.
- Career-specific logbook history.
- Career money and finance systems.
- More consequences for decisions.

## Career Mode has its own save

Career Mode uses a separate Career save.

You can change your displayed Career name in Settings. You can also reset Career progress, but that reset is permanent.

See [Career Mode Settings](/settings-career-mode) before using Reset Career Progress.

## Career Mode pages are separate

Career Mode has its own app pages and workflows. The Free Flight guides for Schedules, Calendar, free-tier airline rotation, and normal bookings should not be treated as Career Mode instructions.

If you are in Career Mode, follow the labels and tasks shown inside Career Mode itself.

## Basic Career Mode flow

1. Start Career Mode.
2. Complete onboarding.
3. Review your current Career status.
4. Use Career dispatch or Career pages to get work.
5. Fly the assigned route.
6. Complete the flight.
7. Review Career progress and consequences.

## Free Flight vs Career Mode

| Topic | Free Flight Mode | Career Mode |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route choice | Flexible schedule booking. | Career work and dispatch rules. |
| Progression | Normal XP and profile stats. | Career-specific progression. |
| Save data | Normal FlyHub account history. | Separate Career save. |
| Reset risk | Normal profile is not reset from Career settings. | Career reset permanently deletes Career progress. |
| Best for | Casual and flexible flying. | Structured roleplay and long-term progression. |
