# General Settings

The General tab controls your public profile, SimBrief defaults, and basic desktop app behavior.

## Change your profile picture

Your profile picture appears across FlyHub.

1. Open Settings.
2. Go to General.
3. Click your avatar or the small camera button.
4. Choose an image from your computer.
5. Wait for the upload to finish.

Supported image types are PNG, JPEG, WEBP, and GIF. The file must be 5 MB or smaller.

## Change your username

Your username is your public display name.

1. Open Settings.
2. Go to General.
3. Find Username.
4. Type the name you want to use.
5. Click Save Changes.

Usernames must be unique. If another pilot already uses that name, choose a different one.

## Manage your plan

If you have a paid plan, the Manage Plan button appears at the top of Settings.

Use Manage Plan to open your FlyHub account page in a browser. From there you can enroll, change your plan, or cancel your subscription.

## SimBrief units

SimBrief Units chooses the default weight units FlyHub sends to SimBrief when generating flight plans.

| Option | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Kilograms (KGS) | Use metric weights. |
| Pounds (LBS) | Use imperial weights. |

Choose the unit system you normally use in your simulator or aircraft.

## SimBrief Pilot ID

The SimBrief Pilot ID is your numeric SimBrief user ID.

You can type it manually in Settings, but FlyHub can also fill it in automatically. When you generate an OFP from My Flights and successfully log in to SimBrief through FlyHub's integration, FlyHub reads the Pilot ID from the new OFP and saves it to your profile.

If the field is empty, you can still generate an OFP. Make sure you log in during the SimBrief flow so the plan is attached to your SimBrief account.

See [Generate a SimBrief OFP](/simbrief-ofp-generation) for the full step-by-step guide.

## Tracking mode

Tracking Mode controls what FlyHub selects when you start a flight.

| Mode | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Ask each time | You want to choose Online or Offline for every flight. |
| Online | You fly on PC with telemetry from MSFS or X-Plane. |
| Offline | You fly on console or want to enter the flight manually. |

Online mode gives full telemetry tracking. Offline mode is for manual completion and earns reduced XP.

## Close to tray

Close to tray changes what happens when you click the X button on the FlyHub Desktop window.

When close to tray is enabled:

1. Clicking X hides FlyHub to the Windows system tray.
2. FlyHub keeps running in the background.
3. Telemetry and background app behavior can continue.
4. You can reopen FlyHub from the tray icon near the clock.

To fully quit FlyHub, right-click the tray icon and choose Quit.

If FlyHub seems like it will not open, check the system tray first. It may already be running.
