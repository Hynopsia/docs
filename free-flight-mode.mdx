# Free Flight Mode

Free Flight Mode is the flexible FlyHub mode.

Use it when you want to choose your own routes, book flights from schedules, build itineraries, fly online or offline, and grow your normal FlyHub profile.

## What you can do

In Free Flight Mode, you can:

- Browse airline schedules.
- Book flights.
- Build multi-leg itineraries.
- Assign flights to calendar dates.
- Start and track flights.
- Recover interrupted flights.
- Complete flights online or offline.
- Earn normal FlyHub XP.
- Build your profile and logbook.

## Free Flight access by plan

Free Flight Mode has different sections depending on your plan.

| Section | Free users | Premium users |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Yes | Yes |
| Schedules | Yes | Yes |
| Calendar | Yes | Yes |
| Tours | Yes | Yes |
| VIP Ops | No | Yes |
| Manual Flight | No | Yes |
| Historical Routes | No | Yes |

Free users have access to:

- Dashboard.
- Schedules.
- Calendar.
- Tours.

Premium users have access to:

- Dashboard.
- Schedules.
- VIP Ops.
- Manual Flight.
- Historical Routes.
- Calendar.
- Tours.

For prices and plan details, see [Pricing and Plans](/pricing-plans).

## Airline access

Free Flight Mode supports both free and premium users.

Free users can fly with five weekly rotating airlines and one monthly airline choice.

The weekly rotation refreshes every Monday at 00:00 UTC. FlyHub selects five eligible airlines for free users, and users can also lock one extra airline for the current UTC month from the Dashboard.

Premium unlocks all airlines and removes those airline rotation limits.

See [Pricing and Plans](/pricing-plans) for the full free rotation and monthly choice explanation.

## Tracking methods

Free Flight Mode supports both tracking methods.

| Tracking style | Use it when |
| --- | --- |
| Online | You fly on PC with MSFS or X-Plane telemetry. |
| Offline | You fly on console or want to enter the flight manually. |

Online flights receive full telemetry-based scoring. Offline flights are manually completed and earn reduced XP.

See [Tracking Methods](/tracking-methods), [Online Tracking](/online-tracking), and [Manual and Offline Mode](/manual-mode) for full guides.

## How a normal Free Flight session works

1. Open Schedules.
2. Book a route or generate an itinerary.
3. Open My Flights.
4. Start the flight.
5. Choose online or offline tracking.
6. Fly the route.
7. Close or finish the flight in FlyHub.
8. Review the result in your logbook.

## What does not apply to Career Mode

Some Free Flight guides are not Career Mode guides.

Schedules, Calendar, free-tier airline rotation, normal bookings, and normal profile XP are Free Flight concepts. Career Mode has its own dispatch, assignments, progression, and Career save.
