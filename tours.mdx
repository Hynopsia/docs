# Tours

Tours are curated sets of flights built around a theme, region, aircraft, airline, or special event.

Use Tours when you want a structured route collection without using Career Mode.

## Browsing tours

Open Tours to browse active, upcoming, and ended tours.

You can search and filter by:

- Tour name.
- Progress.
- Minimum or maximum number of legs.
- Minimum or maximum distance.

Use Reset to clear filters.

## Tour sections

Tours can appear in sections such as:

- Favorites.
- Monthly and Featured.
- Upcoming.
- Ending Soon.
- Aircraft Operations.
- Airline Operations.
- Airport Tours.
- Historical Tours.
- Odyssey Tours.
- Regional Tours.
- Themed Tours.
- VIP Tours.

Ended tours are hidden from the normal Available view unless you filter for ended tours.

## Tour cards

Tour cards can show:

- Cover image.
- Tour status.
- Category.
- Name.
- Description.
- Number of legs.
- Total distance.
- Badge reward.
- Progress if you are enrolled.
- Countdown if the tour ends soon.

You can favorite tours unless the tour type disables favorites.

Monthly tours cannot be favorited.

## Tour detail page

Open a tour to see:

- Tour hero and description.
- Route map.
- Leg list.
- Progress bar.
- Available schedules for a selected leg.
- Leaderboard or pilot table.
- Completed flights.

## Enrolling

1. Open an active tour.
2. Click Enroll to Start.
3. FlyHub starts tracking your progress for that tour.

Upcoming and ended tours cannot be started.

You can leave an enrolled tour from the tour detail page. If you leave, progress tracking stops until you enroll again.

## Leg order

Tours can be sequence-based or any-order.

| Tour type | How legs unlock |
| --- | --- |
| Sequence tour | FlyHub unlocks the next incomplete leg. |
| Any-order tour | All incomplete legs can be active. |

Completed legs are marked complete.

## Booking a tour leg

1. Open a tour.
2. Enroll if you are not already enrolled.
3. Select the leg you want to fly.
4. Review available schedules for that leg.
5. Click Book on the schedule you want.
6. Choose aircraft if FlyHub asks.
7. Confirm the booking.
8. Open My Flights.
9. Start and complete the flight.

Successful bookings go into My Flights.

## Special tour types

Some tours use special booking behavior:

- Historical tours create historical bookings.
- VIP tours create VIP tour bookings.
- Normal tours create normal Free Flight bookings tied to the tour leg.

## Tracking progress

After you complete tour flights, FlyHub updates your tour progress.

The detail page can show:

- Completed legs.
- Remaining legs.
- Progress bar.
- Completed flight records.
- Pilot standings.

## Common mistakes

- Trying to book a leg before enrolling.
- Trying to book an upcoming or ended tour.
- Trying to skip ahead in a sequence-locked tour.
- Forgetting that the booking still needs to be flown from My Flights.
