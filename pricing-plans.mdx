# Pricing and Plans

FlyHub has two main plans: Cadet and Captain.

Cadet is the free plan. Captain is the premium plan.

## Plan prices

| Plan | Price | Best for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cadet | $0/month | Pilots who want basic FlyHub tracking and core Free Flight features. |
| Captain Monthly | $4.99/month | Pilots who want premium features without paying yearly. |
| Captain Yearly | $47.90/year | Pilots who want Captain features for a full year. |

## Cadet plan

Cadet is free.

It includes the core FlyHub experience:

- Basic flight tracking.
- Dashboard access.
- Schedules access.
- Calendar access.
- Tours access.
- Five weekly rotating airlines.
- One monthly personal airline.
- Up to six scheduled flights.
- Unlimited logbook history.
- Standard support.

## Captain plan

Captain is FlyHub Premium.

It unlocks the full Free Flight experience and premium game modes:

- Dashboard access.
- Schedules access.
- VIP Ops access.
- Manual Flight access.
- Historical Routes access.
- Calendar access.
- Tours access.
- Active and historical airlines.
- Historical route planning.
- Career Mode.
- VIP Ops charter mode.
- Unrestricted scheduler.
- Priority support.
- Captain role and profile badge.

That email includes the Captain role benefit and an **Access purchase** button. Use that button to claim the Captain role with the same purchase.

## What free users can do

Free users can use FlyHub without paying.

In Free Flight Mode, free users can access:

- Dashboard.
- Schedules.
- Calendar.
- Tours.

Free users are limited to five weekly rotating airlines and one monthly personal airline.

## Free airline rotation

The Cadet plan includes five weekly rotating airlines.

These airlines are picked for the free tier and shown on your Dashboard. They are the airlines free users can book from during that week.

The weekly rotation works like this:

1. FlyHub selects five active schedulable airlines for free users.
2. The selected airlines appear in the Dashboard free tier section.
3. Free users can book schedules from those airlines.
4. Airlines outside the rotation are locked for free users unless they are your monthly airline choice.
5. The weekly rotation refreshes every Monday at 00:00 UTC.

The weekly airlines are selected randomly from eligible airlines. FlyHub tries to avoid repeating the same airlines from the current rotation when a new random rotation is generated.

If a booked airline is no longer in your weekly rotation or monthly choice, FlyHub may remove pending flights from that locked airline.

## Monthly airline choice

Cadet users also get one monthly airline choice.

This lets you choose one extra airline for the current UTC month. That airline is added on top of the five weekly rotating airlines.

Use it when:

- You want access to a favorite airline that is not in the weekly rotation.
- You want to keep flying one airline across the month.
- You need one more airline option for your route plans.

Important rules:

- You choose the monthly airline from the Dashboard.
- The choice is locked for the current UTC month.
- After it is locked, you cannot keep changing it during that same month.
- When the next UTC month starts, you can choose again.

Example: if your weekly rotation has five airlines and you choose one monthly airline, you can book from six free-tier airlines during that period.

## What premium users can do

Premium users get the full Captain feature set.

In Free Flight Mode, premium users can access:

- Dashboard.
- Schedules.
- VIP Ops.
- Manual Flight.
- Historical Routes.
- Calendar.
- Tours.

Premium also unlocks Career Mode, active and historical airlines, unrestricted scheduling, and priority support.

## Changing or cancelling your plan

You can manage your plan from FlyHub settings.

1. Open FlyHub.
2. Open Settings.
3. Go to General.
4. Select Manage Plan.
5. Use the account page to subscribe, change plans, or cancel.

Plan changes are handled from your FlyHub account page.
