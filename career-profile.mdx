# Career Profile

Career Profile is the record of a Career pilot and its save.

When you open your own profile, it shows the active Career save you are playing. A public link can show a different pilot and save. Use the profile to review the pilot/save you are viewing, including its identity, employer, stats, travel history, type ratings, achievements, and formal career history. Each Career save has its own profile and records. See [Career Saves](/career-saves) for how save selection affects profiles.

## How to access it

Open your profile from the Career sidebar or profile button.

You can also view other pilots from Career leaderboards or community links. Those links open the exact pilot and save represented by the link; viewing another pilot does not change your active save.

Your own Career profile has more private records than public profiles.

## Overview

Overview is the public summary of your Career pilot.

It can show:

- Avatar.
- Career pilot name.
- Current employer.
- Current role.
- Seniority.
- Company position.
- Reputation.
- XP.
- Flights.
- Hours.
- Average score.
- Creator badge if assigned.
- Pinned achievements.
- Pinned type ratings.

Use Overview when you want a quick picture of a pilot's Career status.

## Career Stats

Career Stats shows analytics from Career flights.

It can include:

- Analyzed flights.
- Distance flown.
- Average duration.
- Airports visited.
- Average score.
- Current streak.
- Median landing vertical speed.
- Longest duration.
- Longest flight.
- Best streak.
- Countries visited.
- Top airlines.
- Top aircraft.
- Top routes.
- Monthly activity.
- Online/offline breakdown.

Career Stats uses the Career flight history for the pilot/save you are viewing, not Free Flight history or another Career save.

## Passport

Passport is the Career travel record for the pilot/save you are viewing.

It is built from logged Career flight origins and destinations.

Passport can show:

- Countries visited.
- Interactive world map.
- Visited airports.
- Country stamp grid.
- Airport familiarity badges.

Country stamps are not manually added. They come from completed flights and airport country data.

Airport familiarity badges are based on repeated visits. Higher visit counts can move an airport from explorer-level familiarity toward stronger familiarity tiers.

## Curriculum Vitae

Curriculum Vitae is only available on your own Career profile and describes the active save you are playing.

It is a formal career record and can be exported as a PDF.

The CV can include:

- Pilot identity.
- Nationality.
- Current role.
- Current employer.
- Career start.
- Seniority.
- Reputation.
- Employment history.
- Type rating certifications.
- Contract archive.

Public profiles do not show the CV tab.

## Type ratings

The Type Ratings page shows aircraft families or aircraft ratings tracked for the pilot.

Each rating can show:

- Badge.
- Short code.
- Status.
- Aircraft list.
- Completed flights.
- Last flight.
- Awarded date.
- Requirement details.

Career ratings are based on training and awards for the pilot/save you are viewing.

Common statuses:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Current | Awarded and recently active. |
| Expired | Awarded, but not current from recent activity. |
| In Training | Training is in progress or rating is not yet awarded. |

An expired rating is not deleted. It may need recent flying or simulator recurrency to become current again.

## Achievements and badges

The Achievements page can show:

- Total unlocked or completed badges.
- Pinned badges.
- Creator Partner badge if assigned.
- Career achievements.
- Completed badge rows.

Pinned badges appear on your profile overview.

Achievement pins support up to 8 badges.

For type-rating pins, use what the app allows on the Type Ratings page. Do not assume every rating can be pinned at once.

## Common mistakes

- Opening the normal profile instead of the Career profile.
- Expecting your CV to appear on public profiles.
- Expecting Free Flight stats to appear in Career Stats.
- Expecting passport stamps to be manual.
- Treating expired type ratings as lost.
- Expecting Career achievements to use Free Flight tour badges.
