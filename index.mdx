## First Flight Checklist

1. Create your FlyHub account.
2. Install FlyHub Desktop if you fly on PC.
3. Book a route from Schedules or My Flights.
4. Start the flight in FlyHub before departure.
5. Fly normally in your simulator.
6. Close the flight in FlyHub after landing and taxi-in.

## Supported simulator modes

| Platform | Best FlyHub mode | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020/2024 on PC | Online telemetry | Uses SimConnect through the desktop app. |
| X-Plane 12 on PC | Online telemetry | Requires the FlyHub X-Plane plugin. |
| Xbox or PlayStation | Manual/offline | Log the flight manually after completion. |
| PC without telemetry | Manual/offline | Useful if you do not want automatic tracking. |

## Plan basics

FlyHub can be used for free with the Cadet plan.

Cadet users can access Dashboard, Schedules, Calendar, and Tours in Free Flight Mode. Captain users get premium access, including VIP Ops, Manual Flight, Historical Routes, Career Mode, active and historical airlines, unrestricted scheduling, and priority support.

See [Pricing and Plans](/pricing-plans) for the full comparison.
