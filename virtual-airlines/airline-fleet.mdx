# Airline Profile: Fleet

The **Fleet** tab is a public, read-only view of the airline's active aircraft. It helps pilots understand what an airline operates and where its aircraft are positioned.

## Search and filters

Search or filter by:

- registration;
- aircraft name;
- aircraft type;
- current location;
- airline;
- purpose or operating status.

The list shows up to 30 aircraft per page. Group profiles can include the group's fleet; use the airline filter when it is available to focus on an operating airline.

## Aircraft details

Open an aircraft to see its photo or fallback image, registration, name, type, operator, hours, cycles, completed flights, station, purpose, condition, status, ownership, and a short list of recent flights.

Only aircraft that are not retired or closed are included in this public view. The profile does not provide actions to edit, transfer, retire, lease, or maintain an aircraft.

## Empty states and refresh

Anyone can browse this public view without joining the airline or having fleet-management permission. If no aircraft match the filters, clear them or try a broader search. If the list cannot load, reload the profile.

The public profile is a summary for browsing. For the complete operational picture, use the airline's management Fleet page when you have access.
