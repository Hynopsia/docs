# Manage Airline: People

Use **People** to manage membership applications, the active roster, staff roles, and pilot activity. This is an administrative page; the public profile's [Pilots](./airline-pilots) tab is read-only.

## Open People

1. Open an active airline from **Airlines**.
2. Open its profile and select **Manage airline**.
3. Select **People**.

People uses the **shared group roster**. Applications, pilots, and staff roles belong to the group when the airline is part of a group, even though each operating airline keeps its own aircraft, routes, schedules, and flight performance.

People is available to active members with People access. Owner, Manager, and Pilots Manager roles normally have it. Pilot-only roles cannot manage People. Membership actions require membership-management access, and assigning or revoking staff roles requires role-management access.

## Pilot overview

The **Pilot overview** tab summarizes:

- active pilots;
- pending applications;
- pilots inactive for 30 days or more;
- staff members.

It can also show roster activity, pilot scores, **Airline XP** by pilot, rank distribution, membership status, pilot performance history, and participation history. Use the 30-day, 90-day, one-year, or founding range when available. Scores and participation are based on accepted VA flights.

## Applications

Applications contains pending membership requests. Select an applicant to review their profile, accepted flights, block time, score, strikes, Airline XP, current applicant rank, application date, last flight, and inactivity information.

### Accept an application

1. Select a pending application.
2. Review the applicant's profile and available flight history.
3. Select **Accept application**.
4. Confirm **Accept pilot**.

Acceptance activates the membership, records the current policy version, creates the pilot's VA statistics, grants the Pilot role, and sends the welcome notification through Inbox. Acceptance does not ask for a separate reason.

### Reject an application

Select **Reject**, provide a reason of at least three characters, and confirm. The application becomes rejected and the reason is recorded.

## Roster actions

The roster includes active and suspended members. Search by name or rank and use **Previous** and **Next** to browse 25 pilots per page. The number at the end of each row is **days idle**: days since the pilot's last flight or airline activity.

**Airline XP** on a pilot record is the XP that pilot has earned on this airline's flights. Migrated hours never add to it.

- **Suspend** an active pilot with a reason.
- **Restore** a suspended pilot; the interface asks for a reason.
- **Remove** a suspended member with a reason.

The page may require an active pilot to be suspended before it can be removed. Ending a membership revokes its active staff grants.

## Strikes and automatic moderation

A pilot's record shows **Last VA flight N days ago** (completed or diverted VA flights anywhere in the group).

When the airline turns on automatic moderation, the record also shows the rules that apply to that pilot:

- **Strikes** appear as "N of M", where M is the next step (suspension or removal). A strike is an accepted flight scored under the airline's warning rating. Strikes reset each UTC month; the record names the month being counted (for example, October 2026).
- **Inactivity** — the pilot is removed automatically after the set number of days without a completed VA flight.
- **Monthly minimum** — the record shows how many VA flights the pilot has this month against the minimum; pilots below it are removed at month end.

The Owner is never auto-moderated. With automatic moderation off, none of these rules run. Marking a low score as reviewed in [Operations](./manage-operations) does not remove a strike.

## Ranks and staff roles

**Ranks** is a read-only view showing pilot counts, flights, hours, and average score by rank. Rank requirements, promotions, honorary ranks, and rank locks are managed in [Rewards](./manage-rewards); use **Edit ranks in Rewards** if your role includes Rewards.

**Staff roles** lists every active member who holds a staff role, with the same total shown on the tab, and the sections each role includes. Users with role-management access can **Grant** or **Revoke** an eligible role for an active member. The Owner role cannot be changed from People; ownership is managed in [Ownership](./manage-ownership).

## Limits and safeguards

The standard roster view requests 25 rows and supports 10–50 rows per page. A free shared roster supports up to 20 active pilots; the Founder must unlock more capacity through the available plan before the limit can be exceeded. Admission still follows the airline's published requirements.

Actions that change membership or roles require confirmation. Revocations and destructive membership actions require a reason. FlyHub protects repeated submissions, so wait for the result before submitting the same action again.

## Loading, empty, and error states

If no airline is selected, choose one from the management workspace. If your role does not include People, ask the Founder or Manager for access. Each list has its own empty message, such as **No applications waiting** or **No staff yet**, and a search with no results says so.

If a list or history fails to load, refresh the relevant view or reload the page. A temporary loading problem should not be interpreted as a membership decision. Follow the recovery instruction shown by the page.
