# Manage Airline: Airports

Use **Airports** to build the airport network that your airline can use for routes and schedules. In an airline group, the airport network is shared: adding a station makes it available to the group's operating airlines, while each operating airline still keeps its own identity, fleet, routes, and schedules.

## Open Airports

1. Open an active airline from **Airlines**.
2. Open its profile and select **Manage airline**.
3. Select **Airports**.

Airport management requires the appropriate network and financial access. The page can be read-only for staff who can view the network but cannot change it. You need an active airline or group context before adding or changing a station.

## Airport network overview

The summary shows active airports, hubs used out of 10, **Airport fees** (what the next nightly charge takes, after the 25% rate for quiet airports, with the full rate alongside), and airline cash right now.

Right under it, three short notes explain what each role gives you: an airport in your network, a hub, and the home airport.

Each row in the list shows:

- **Code**: your own short code for the airport;
- ICAO code, name, city, and country;
- **Size**: small, medium, or large, which sets the costs below;
- **Flights, 30 days**: completed departures and arrivals at the airport by any airline in your group over the last 30 days;
- **Fee per night**: what the airport is actually charged. A quiet airport shows the reduced amount and "Quiet · 25% of" the full rate;
- **Role**: Home, Hub, or Removed.

Filter by airport name, ICAO, IATA, or your code, and choose active airports, hubs only, removed airports, or all. The list shows 20 airports per page. Open an airport's statistics or gate assignments from its row. You can have up to 10 hubs.

## Add an airport station

1. Select **Add airport**.
2. Search for the airport and pick it. FlyHub fills in the next free code; you can change it.
3. Review whether it will be the home base or a hub.
4. Review the setup and recurring cost.
5. Confirm the addition.

The code is a short label you choose: 2–4 letters or numbers, unique within the group. It appears in the Code column and works in the filter. The airport must be valid for the current network and must meet the page's eligibility checks. Adding a station charges the current setup cost; setting it as a hub adds the hub cost.

Current cost bands are:

| Airport size | Setup | Additional hub cost | Daily station cost | Daily hub cost |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Small | FH$20,000 | FH$40,000 | FH$160 | FH$280 |
| Medium | FH$50,000 | FH$100,000 | FH$400 | FH$800 |
| Large | FH$100,000 | FH$200,000 | FH$800 | FH$2,000 |

Daily station and hub costs are taken from airline cash every night at 00:10 UTC, even below zero. An airport other than the home airport that has been in the network for more than 14 days and has had no completed flight by your group in the last 14 days is charged 25% until the next flight. The Fee per night column and the Airport fees figure already include this.

If you cannot afford a change or have hit the hub limit, the confirm button stays disabled and the page says why, including how much cash is missing. The form is the final authority if the current economy or account rules change.

## Edit, remove, or restore a station

Open an airport and select **Edit** to update its network details or hub status. A home base must be an active station in the shared network before it can be selected as the airline's home airport.

Select **Remove** only when the airport is no longer needed. You can only remove it if no flying or paused schedule uses it; retire or delete that schedule in Network first. Past flights and free flights to or from the airport don't block removal. Removing an airport stops its nightly fee and keeps its past flights and statistics.

To add a removed airport back, show **Removed airports**, edit it, and pay the one-time cost again at the current price.

## Group behavior and related pages

**Airport charges** means the handling charge on departure and the landing charge on arrival. An airport in your network takes 12% off airport charges. A hub takes 25% off airport charges and 15% off departure fuel cost. Departing from an airport outside your network costs 25% more fuel and gets no discount.

Airports and their treasury impact are shared by the group. Routes and schedules are still managed for an individual operating airline in [Network](./manage-network). Use [Identity and Profile](./manage-identity-profile) to change an airline's home base after the required station exists. The new home becomes a hub, and the old home stays a paying hub unless you are already at 10 hubs.

Adding or editing a station can require both network and financial access even when the visible form mainly looks like a network task. A station change does not automatically create routes or aircraft assignments.

## Empty states and recovery

If no airports are configured, add a valid station before creating routes or an operating airline that needs a shared home airport. If the search returns no results, try the full airport name or code and clear any status filter.

If an airport cannot be added or edited, check the code, available group funds, required permissions, active group state, and whether the station is already used by a current schedule. If a change fails, reload Airports and verify the station's status before trying again. Do not assume a failed confirmation changed the network.
