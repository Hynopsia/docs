| Product | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Free / Cadet | VA access, participation, and ownership of one non-closed airline. |
| Premium / Captain | Personal features and ownership of up to three non-closed airlines, including operating airlines. |
| Airline plan | A larger shared active roster for one airline or group: USD 15/month plus applicable tax. |

All airlines include **20 active members** without the airline plan. The owner counts when active; pending applications and inactive memberships do not. The airline plan is a flat monthly subscription, not a per-pilot charge. Personal Premium and the airline plan do not replace each other. Explicitly exempt and system airlines follow their assigned plan.

| Active roster without the airline plan | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 19 | The next pilot can join free. |
| 20 | The airline remains operational free. The airline plan is required for pilot 21. |
| 21 or more | Every airline in the group is suspended: no new flights or admissions until the plan is active or the roster is back to 20. |

## Groups

The flagship holds the shared subscription and roster. Operating airlines use that coverage; they do not receive a separate allowance of 20 or incur another airline plan charge. Each still counts toward the owner's limit of one Free or three Premium non-closed airlines.

## Checkout and cancellation

The owner opens **Manage airline → Ownership** to start the airline plan or open the billing portal. New purchases use Paddle. Review the currency, applicable taxes, recurring monthly total, and cancellation terms displayed before paying. Existing subscriptions linked to another supported provider retain their provider's management path.

Use the billing portal to manage payment details and cancellation. If it belongs to a different payer or the portal cannot be opened, contact [FlyHub Support](mailto:admin@flyhub.app). Do not start another subscription while confirmation is pending.

A scheduled cancellation retains coverage until the effective end of the active period. Overdue payments have **seven days of grace**; if the grace period ends unpaid, every airline in the group is suspended. Paused, ended, or refunded subscriptions do not provide capacity above the free limit. The dates and status in Ownership show your current coverage.

## What remains after the airline plan ends

At 20 active pilots or fewer, free operation remains available. Above 20 without coverage, every airline in the group is suspended: new flights and admissions stop until payment or roster capacity is resolved.

Members, pending applications, balances, reputation, completed history, and existing reservations are preserved. Accepted reservations continue under normal flight, membership, recovery, expiry, and safety rules. An itinerary without a reservation is not an accepted booking. Nobody is automatically expelled to reduce the roster.

Restoring coverage or reducing the active roster to 20 or fewer can restore billing-suspended operations. Separate moderation restrictions are unaffected. Ending personal Premium also preserves existing airlines; only additional creation is limited.

Retiring an airline is a separate, permanent action with an explicit impact summary. It is not required to cancel the airline plan. See [Ownership](./manage-ownership) and [terms](https://flyhub.app/legal).
