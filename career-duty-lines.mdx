# Career Duty Lines

A Duty Line is an optional, ready-to-fly shift in Career Mode. It links **2–4 flights** together for one aircraft. Fly them in order, keep an eye on the finish deadline, and bring the aircraft back to the line's final airport.

Unlike a regular Dispatch itinerary, a Duty Line is booked as one job. Once you book it, its flights appear together in [Career My Flights](/career-my-flights); you do not need to file them on the Calendar first. The Calendar can still show the line's progress on your Career schedule.

## Build a line

1. Open **Career → Dispatch** and turn on **Duty Line**.
2. Choose **2, 3, or 4 legs**. A leg is one flight.
3. Choose how the route should finish:
   - **Continuous** keeps moving on to the next airport.
   - **Return to base** brings you back to the airport where the line began.
4. Choose one eligible aircraft. You need to be allowed to fly that aircraft for your current Career airline.
5. Set any airport filters you want, then choose your pace and, if useful, a time cap of **4, 6, or 8 hours**. You can also choose **No cap**.
6. Select **Generate Duty Line**. Review the route, times, and reward before you book.

Your line starts at your **current Career location**. Generating or reviewing a line does not start its clock. You can look through new offers and generate another one if the route does not suit you. Each offer is available for 10 minutes, so book the one you want before it expires.

The time cap is a limit, not a target: for example, **Keep under 6 hours** asks for a line that fits within six hours. Choose **No cap** to let the selected legs and route determine the length. If no line is available, try fewer legs, another eligible aircraft, wider airport filters, or a higher time cap.

## Choose your pace

| Pace | Maximum duty bonus | What to expect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tight** | Up to 20% | Less breathing room between flights and a 10-minute finish buffer. |
| **Comfortable** | Up to 15% | More time between flights and a 20-minute finish buffer. |

The aircraft also has a minimum time on the ground between flights. Some larger aircraft need a longer minimum turn. The offer shows the turn and finish buffer for the line you are considering.

## Understand the clock before booking

Times in the offer are shown relative to booking. **T+0** means the moment you select **Book duty · start clock**. The first departure is planned for **T+40 minutes**, giving you a report window to get ready and start the first flight. The offer's **Finish by** time is the deadline for arriving from the last flight.

The total estimate includes time to prepare for the first flight, the planned flights, time on the ground between them, and the final recovery buffer. The recovery buffer is already included in the displayed deadline; it is not extra time after that deadline.

When you confirm the booking:

- The clock starts and the line's timetable is set.
- Start online tracking for the first flight within the 40-minute report window.
- Fly the legs in the order shown and respect the minimum turn between them.
- Finish the last flight by the displayed deadline to qualify for the full finish portion of the bonus.

The deadline uses real UTC time. Pausing your simulator, closing the app, or changing Career saves does not pause or extend it. The offer's times are only a preview; the actual line clock starts when you book.

## Fly and follow your progress

After booking, open **My Flights** to see the line, its next leg, and the deadline. Start each flight from there and choose online tracking. Duty Lines require online tracking for every leg so FlyHub can check the route, timing, and landing score. Offline or manual completion cannot earn the Duty Line bonus.

Fly each planned route, complete the flight, and allow the displayed minimum ground time before continuing. My Flights and the Calendar update as you make progress. The projected arrival is a guide based on your flights so far; your actual arrival and remaining time can change as you fly.

## How the pay works

You receive normal Career flight pay for each completed leg through your usual payday. The **Duty Line bonus is one extra payment for the whole line** after all of its flights have been checked. It is not a separate bonus for each leg.

The bonus is worth up to **20% of the eligible flight pay on a Tight line** or **15% on a Comfortable line**. For example, if the eligible flight pay for the whole line is **$1,000**, the maximum extra bonus is **$200 on Tight** or **$150 on Comfortable**. Your actual bonus depends on how close each flight is to its planned time and when you reach the final airport.

The bonus has two parts:

- **40%** depends on the timing of each leg. Depart within 15 minutes of its planned time and arrive no more than 15 minutes late to earn that leg's share.
- **60%** depends on the final arrival. Arrive by the deadline to earn this part in full. If you finish late, this part gets smaller and reaches zero 30 minutes after the deadline.

If you finish late, you can still keep the punctual-leg part you earned. Ordinary lateness does not take away reputation. A Duty Line's bonus replaces the normal Calendar-date bonus for those flights.

To earn the bonus, every leg must use online tracking, follow its planned route, meet the minimum turn, score at least **7.5/10**, and pass the usual Career flight checks. If a leg misses one of these checks, the line may no longer qualify for its bonus.

## Results and reputation

- **Finish by the deadline:** You can earn the full bonus and **+0.10 reputation** when all flight checks pass.
- **Clean Line:** Every leg meets its timing target and you finish by the deadline.
- **Comeback:** You fall more than 10 minutes behind on a leg, then recover enough to finish the line by its deadline.
- **Finish late:** The deadline part of the bonus shrinks; there is no extra reputation penalty just for being late.
- **End the line early:** Keep normal pay for completed flights. You do not receive the Duty Line bonus, and ending early does not reduce reputation.
- **Miss the first report window:** If you have not started the first flight by the 40-minute report deadline, the line is a missed report. You lose the Duty Line bonus and **0.20 reputation**.
