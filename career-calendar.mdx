# Career Calendar

Career Calendar is the roster planning page.

After Dispatch files assignments, Calendar is where you place them on dates and lock the roster.

## What Calendar is for

Use Calendar to:

- View accepted assignments.
- Drag pending flights onto dates.
- Auto assign editable flights.
- Check scheduled flights.
- Lock the roster.
- Review completed flights.

## Normal workflow

1. File assignments from Dispatch.
2. Open Calendar.
3. Drag pending flights onto today or a future date.
4. Use Auto Assign if you want FlyHub to distribute editable flights.
5. Make sure each required flight has a date.
6. Click Complete Roster.
7. Open My Flights.
8. Start the Career flights from My Flights.

## Roster lock

Completing the roster locks the plan.

Locked flights can move to Career My Flights and become flyable.

After a roster is locked, assignments cannot always be rearranged. Check dates before confirming.

## Date rules

- You cannot place new assignments on past dates.
- Locked assignments cannot be rearranged like editable assignments.
- Calendar validates dispatch order before locking.
- Some assignments must be flown in order.

## On-time bonus

Career Calendar can show an on-time bonus rule.

To earn the cash bonus, fly the assignment on the scheduled date.

Flying off-schedule may still complete the flight, but it can forfeit the on-time bonus.

## Common mistakes

- Filing assignments from Dispatch and forgetting to open Calendar.
- Dragging flights onto past dates.
- Forgetting to click Complete Roster.
- Expecting unlocked Calendar assignments to appear in My Flights.
- Flying on the wrong date and losing the on-time bonus.
- Trying to rearrange flights after they are locked.
